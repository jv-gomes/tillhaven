# Tillhaven — full QA, gameplay and visual audit

**Date:** 2026-09-03 · **Build:** working tree at `67081e4` + uncommitted Phases 15–17
**Method:** live playthrough in Chromium (1440×900 and five other viewports) driven over CDP,
plus source inspection. Fresh account `qaudit`, registered in-session. Every finding below was
observed in the running game unless it is explicitly marked *code-read only*.

Findings marked **FIXED** were resolved during or after the audit (BUG-01/02/03 in the audit
itself; BUG-04 and BUG-06 in the follow-up). Everything else is reported only.

---

## A. Executive summary

Tillhaven is a **well-built game with a serious presentation problem**. The engineering is
genuinely strong: server-authoritative throughout, 1,432 passing tests, clean transaction and
idempotency handling, no console errors anywhere in a full playthrough, no memory leak across 10
scene transitions, and a steady frame rate. Every economic action I tried — buy, sell, ship,
upgrade, harvest, place — did the right thing, refused the right things, and reported machine-
readable errors with human-readable toasts.

What lets it down is almost entirely *what the player sees*:

1. **The character was being erased by its own farm.** Standing on any plot, the opaque tilled-soil
   tile drew over the player from every position and every approach direction, reducing the
   character to a floating head during the game's core activity. Inside the house the player
   vanished entirely behind furniture. This was a category error in the depth model, not a crop
   bug. **Fixed** — see §E.
2. **The game renders at 1× on the most common laptop resolution**, filling ~16% of the screen with
   the rest dark blue. At 1366×768 it misses 2× zoom by 0.012.
3. **Animals stand nowhere near their buildings.** Chickens appear in a row at the top edge of the
   map, six rows north of the coop and partly behind the HUD; the first cow spawns in the far
   bottom-left corner, 19 tiles from its barn.
4. **The drawn path is not walkable.** The mailbox, merchant stall and shipping box are all solid
   and all sit on the y=13 path run, severing it in three places.

None of these are hard to fix. The game is close.

### BUG-01 — Tilled soil and crops draw over the player · **H** · Rendering · **FIXED**

**Repro:** Till and plant any plot. Walk onto it from any direction, at any growth stage.
**Expected:** The character stands on the soil; the crop occludes only when the player is further
up the tile than the plant's base.
**Actual:** The opaque 16×16 soil tile drew over the character from *every* position on the tile.
At mid-tile the character was reduced to a head with no body (screenshot `09d-between-crops`).
**Root cause:** `Farm.ts` put soil + outline + crop in one container sorted at
`groundDepth(top + TILE_SIZE)` — the tile's *bottom* edge. A player standing on that tile has feet
in `(top, top+TILE_SIZE]`, so their depth was always `<=` the container's. At an exact tie, Phaser's
stable sort handed the win to the plot, which is created after the player.
**Fix:** New `DEPTH.groundDecal` band for flat paint-on-the-floor objects; crop split out to sort on
its own feet; `CHARACTER_BIAS` breaks ties in the character's favour. Full write-up in §E.
**Difficulty:** M · **Regression risk:** Low (12 new unit tests pin the invariants).

### BUG-02 — Player vanishes entirely behind interior furniture · **H** · Rendering · **FIXED**

**Repro:** Enter the house, buy and place the Four-poster Bed, stand on its footprint.
**Actual:** The player was completely invisible (screenshot `29c-bed-placed`) — furniture is
walk-through by design and was drawn over the whole character.
**Root cause:** Same as BUG-01. `Interior.ts` also inlined `DEPTH.world + cell.y` instead of calling
`groundDepth` — the last surviving copy of the formula T-15.29 collapsed.
**Fix:** `CHARACTER_BIAS` now puts the player in front at the piece's own ground line, and the
inline formula was replaced with `groundDepth`. Standing *further up* a tall piece still draws the
player behind it, which is correct for an upright object.
**Residual:** A player standing on the back row of a 2-row piece is still hidden. The clean fix is
to make furniture base-rows solid, reusing `modules/decor/reachability.ts`'s flood fill so a player
cannot seal themselves in. Left as a roadmap item — it is a design call, not a bug fix.

### BUG-03 — Faced-tile highlight invisible on tilled soil · **M** · Rendering · **FIXED**

**Repro:** Face any tilled plot. **Actual:** No outline. **Expected:** The outline is the entire
feedback mechanism for "this is what the action key will hit".
**Root cause:** The target rectangle sat on `DEPTH.decor` (−1900) with a comment claiming it was
"above the ground and the soil". It had been under the soil ever since the plot became a per-plot
sprite. Ironically, moving the soil into the decal band would have *kept* it hidden.
**Fix:** New `DEPTH.targetOutline` band between the decals and the world sprites.

### BUG-04 — Camera renders at 1× on common laptop resolutions · **H** · Camera/Visual · **FIXED**

**Repro:** Open the game at 1366×768. **Actual:** `zoom = 1`; the 480×352 map occupies 16% of a
1366×768 window, the rest flat `#123040`. **Expected:** the largest integer zoom that fits.
**Measured:**

| Viewport | zoom | map on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1920×1080 | 2 | 960×704 |
| 1440×900 | 2 | 960×704 |
| **1366×768** | **1** | **480×352** |
| 1024×600 | 1 | 480×352 |
| 390×844 | 1 | 480×352 (wider than the screen) |

**Root cause:** `Farm.fitCamera` (`apps/client/src/game/scenes/Farm.ts:852`) computes
`fit = min(width/mapW, (height − hudBar)/mapH)` then `Math.floor`. At 1366×768 that is
`min(2.85, 1.988) → floor → 1`. It misses zoom 2 by **0.012** — about four pixels of viewport
height. `Math.floor` with no tolerance throws away nearly half the available scale.
**Recommended fix:** the real decision is D-12, still open. Two options:
 - *Cheap:* allow a small tolerance (`Math.floor(fit + 0.05)`) and reduce the HUD margin the fit
   subtracts. Buys 1366×768 immediately, changes nothing else.
 - *Correct:* a follow camera with `setBounds`, so zoom is chosen to fill the viewport and the
   camera tracks the player. This also fixes the mobile crop (BUG-05) and stops the farm being a
   postage stamp on large monitors. It is what the genre does, and it is what D-12 is about.
**Difficulty:** S (tolerance) / M (follow camera) · **Regression risk:** Low / Medium.
**Fixed (T-18.03), by a third option neither of the above describes:** fit the map *minus its
one-tile decorative frame* and reserve **both** HUD bars instead of only the top one, with compact
chrome on viewports under 820px tall. 1366×768 now renders at zoom 2 with nothing hidden behind
either bar; 1920×1080 and 1440×900 are unchanged. **1280×720 stays at zoom 1** — twenty tile rows
need 640px and 720 minus even the compact chrome is 606, so it genuinely cannot be bought without
hiding content or a follow camera. D-12 therefore remains open, and 720p is now the case that needs
it. The arithmetic moved to `game/camera.ts` with 13 tests.

### BUG-05 — Unplayable below ~1000px wide · **H** · Responsive

**Repro:** 390×844. **Actual:** The HUD bar overflows — `DECORATE`, `IDLE` and `LOG OUT` are off
screen and unreachable, `BAG` is clipped. The 480px map is wider than the viewport and cropped on
both sides with no camera follow and no pan. The hotbar gets its own horizontal scrollbar. There is
no touch input of any kind, so the character cannot be moved regardless.
**Note:** mobile may be deliberately out of scope, but nothing in `ROADMAP.md` says so either way,
and the landing page markets "plant before work, harvest at lunch". Worth an explicit decision.
**Difficulty:** M–L · **Depends on:** BUG-04's camera decision.

### BUG-06 — Animals stand nowhere near their buildings · **H** · World layout · **FIXED**

**Repro:** Buy a chicken and a cow. **Actual:** Chickens render in a row at tile y=1, at the very
top edge of the map, partly behind the HUD bar. The coop is at tiles y=7–11 — six empty rows away.
The first cow stands at tile (2,19), the far bottom-left of the map; the barn is at x=21–25,
bottom-right. Confirmed by sprite coordinates and by screenshot `22-whole-farm`.
**Root cause:** two separate problems in `packages/shared/src/config/pasture.ts`:
 - `COOP_YARD.origin = {x:21, y:1}` is pinned so that it clears the coop at its **largest** tier.
   `COOP_FOOTPRINT` is the Deluxe footprint — x21–29, **y4–11** — so the yard's comment ("the three
   rows above the Deluxe coop's roof, `COOP_FOOTPRINT.y0` is 4") is *correct*, and rows 1–3 are the
   only band that is clear of the coop at every tier. The bug is the consequence: a **tier-0**
   player's coop occupies y7–11, so their chickens stand three empty rows north of it, hard against
   the map's top border. One fixed yard cannot hug a building that grows.
   *(An earlier draft of this audit called the comment stale by comparing the yard against the
   tier-0 footprint from the live block map rather than against `COOP_FOOTPRINT`. It is not stale.)*
 - `BARN_YARD` is a 9-wide row starting at x=2 and filled left-to-right, so with one cow the herd
   is placed at the point *furthest* from the barn. That is the authored design, and the design is
   wrong: fill order should start nearest the building.
 - `pastureFitsFarm()` only asserts the yards are inside the map — never that they are adjacent to
   the building they belong to, which is why this passed 9 tests.
**Recommended fix:** make the coop yard follow the **current** tier's roofline instead of the
largest tier's clearance, fill both yards from the building outward, and reserve the union of every
tier's yard so an upgrade cannot move a yard onto placed decor. **Difficulty:** S–M ·
**Regression risk:** Medium — `pastureSlot` is also read by the server's decor reachability.
**Fixed (T-18.04)** exactly as recommended. `Yard.spacing` is signed so a yard fills away from its
building; `coopYard(tier)` derives the chicken rows from the current roof; `yardTilesAcrossTiers()`
is the union that `reservedFarmTiles` and the server's `checkReachable` now reserve. Verified live:
chickens sit on the roofline at every tier and follow it when the coop is upgraded mid-session, and
the first cow stands beside the barn.

### BUG-07 — The drawn path is severed in three places · **M** · World layout / Collision

**Repro:** Stand on the path at (8,13) and hold ← or →.
**Actual:** Walking west stops at x=101 (tile 6); walking east stops at x=219 (tile 13). Confirmed
by position readout, not inference.
**Root cause:** `OBJ_COLLISION_BASE` makes the mailbox (3,13), the merchant stall (5,13) and the
shipping box (14,13) solid, and `PATHS` includes the run `[3,13,20,13]`. The objects were
deliberately placed *on* the path so the player passes them — but they block it, so the player must
detour onto the grass. `assertPathsClearOfBuildings` validates buildings only, never map objects.
**Recommended fix:** move the three objects one tile off the run (y=12 or y=14) and extend
`assertPathsClearOfBuildings` to cover `OBJECT_TILES` with their measured footings, so this cannot
recur. Note the merchant NPC added in this audit was deliberately placed at (7,12) — off the run —
and a test now pins that. **Difficulty:** S–M · **Regression risk:** Medium (moving map objects
means editing `farm.json`, which must not be regenerated — see D-13).

### BUG-08 — Harvesting fills your hand and blocks the next harvest · **M** · Inventory/UX

**Repro:** Select an empty hotbar slot. Harvest a ripe crop whose produce does not stack into an
existing slot. Harvest the next one.
**Actual:** `Empty your hands to harvest by hand.` The produce landed in the first free backpack
slot, which was the equipped slot, so the hand is no longer empty. Verified end-to-end: slot 6 was

**Recommended fix:** either let a harvest-by-hand succeed while holding *harvestable produce* (the

equipped one. The first is smaller and reads better. **Difficulty:** S · **Regression risk:** Low —
`actions.ts`'s decision matrix is pure and well covered.

## E. Sprite layering investigation

### The question asked

*Why do crops render above the player, and is it isolated or systemic?*

### Answer: systemic, and the crop was not even the worst part

The scene sorts every standing sprite by its **feet Y** — `groundDepth(feetY)`, in
`apps/client/src/game/depth.ts`. That model is correct and does not need replacing. Two things
violated it.

**1. Flat ground was sorted as if it were standing up.**

`Farm.syncTiles` put three objects into one container — an opaque 16×16 soil image, the plot
outline, and the crop sprite — and `Farm.layout` gave that container
`groundDepth(top + TILE_SIZE)`, the tile's *bottom* edge.

A player standing on that tile has feet anywhere in `(top, top + TILE_SIZE]`. So:

```
plot row 9:   top = 144, container depth = 160
player feet:  y = 150   → depth 150  →  drawn UNDER the plot
player feet:  y = 160   → depth 160  →  tie → plot wins (created later)
```

There is no position on the tile where the player won. Verified live from all four approach
directions: `covered: true` at y = 151.5, 152.7, 152.7 and 152.7.

The crop being visible on top is the part you notice. The part that actually erased the character
was the **soil** — a full opaque tile with no transparency. Soil is paint on the floor. It has no
height. It must never occlude anything standing on it, and giving it a feet-Y implied it could.

**2. Exact ties resolved by scene construction order.**

Phaser stable-sorts, so equal depths keep display-list order. That made draw order depend on the
order `Farm.create()` happens to build things — a rule documented in exactly one comment
(`// Before the player, so a character walking past the door draws in front.`) and enforced nowhere.
Buildings are built before the player and won their ties; plots, animals and decor are built after
and lost theirs.

**Reach beyond crops:** interior furniture (worst case — the player disappeared completely),
walk-through decor such as the berry bush, and any future tall object added to the farm's currently
empty `decor` tile layer, which sits at `DEPTH.decor` and would therefore draw *behind* the player
even when the player is north of it.

### The fix

Two changes to `apps/client/src/game/depth.ts`, and their call sites.

**A ground-decal band.** `DEPTH.groundDecal = -1000` — one fixed depth for flat things painted on
the floor. Decals never overlap each other (one per tile), so relative order among them is
meaningless and a single value is enough. `Farm.ts` now splits the plot: soil + outline go into a
`decal` container pinned there; the crop becomes a standalone sprite sorting at
`groundDepth(top + TILE_SIZE)` like any other upright object.

That keeps the *correct* half of the old behaviour: a player standing in the back half of a tile
still draws behind the plant, because they genuinely are behind it.

**A character bias.** `CHARACTER_BIAS = 0.5`, applied by `characterDepth()` /
`characterShadowDepth()` in `Player.ts` and `Animal.ts`. Strictly less than 1, so it can never push
a character past the next pixel row, and `aboveGround`/`belowGround` keep bracketing their own
sprite. When a character and a fence post touch the ground on the same line, the character wins —
which is always the right answer, and it removes the dependency on construction order entirely.

A third band, `DEPTH.targetOutline = -900`, fixes BUG-03 as a consequence: the faced-tile highlight
now sits above the decals and below everything that stands.

### Why not the alternatives

- *"Raise the player's z-index"* — reintroduces exactly the fixed-layer thinking the feet-Y model
  exists to avoid; the player would then draw over trees they are standing behind.
- *A per-frame scene-wide Y-sort pass* — Phaser already sorts by depth every frame; adding a second
  pass costs time and buys nothing. Only three things need re-sorting per frame (the player, its
  shadow, and animals) and they already do it.
- *Making everything solid so overlap cannot happen* — D-9 and D-14 both rejected this for good
  reasons (a walk-through berry pile, chickens that would wall in their own yard), and it would not
  help the soil, which must stay walkable.

## F. Gameplay improvements (highest impact first)

**F-1 — The first hour has a progression cliff.** A new player has 500g. A chicken costs 400g and
takes **12 hours to mature** and ~13.5 h to first produce. Buying one leaves 100g, which buys
nothing — the cheapest seed is 20g but only 6 plots are unlocked and 3 already have crops. Within
five minutes the player has: three crops growing (45 min–2 h), one baby chicken (12 h), 100 gold and
nothing to do. Recommend a fast first loop — a cheap fast crop, or a starting hen that is already
mature, or a first-harvest milestone reward.

**F-2 — Watering is invisible as a requirement.** A dry crop pauses forever and the only signal is
the hover countdown reading `DRY · …`. A player who does not hover will never learn why nothing
grows. Recommend a persistent visual on dry planted soil (the wet/dry sprites exist) and a HUD count
of thirsty plots.

**F-3 — Fix BUG-08.** Harvesting a row currently requires cycling the hotbar mid-row.

**F-4 — Fix BUG-09 and BUG-10.** Idle mode's headline feature can be switched on into a state where
it provably does nothing, and panels do not take input.

**F-5 — There is no tutorial or first-run guidance.** The action key is never named. A new player is
dropped onto a farm with a hoe and no prompt. Even one line of onboarding copy at the first plot
would carry the whole loop. (T-14.10.)

**F-6 — No feedback on action impact.** Tilling, watering and harvesting all play a swing animation
and a toast, but nothing happens *at the tile* — no dust, no splash, no pop. This is the single
cheapest way to make the loop feel better (see G-4).

## G. Visual improvements (highest impact first)

**G-1 — Fix the camera (BUG-04).** Nothing else on this list changes the impression the game makes
as much as not rendering at 1× in a sea of dark blue.

**G-2 — Fix animal placement (BUG-06).** Livestock scattered to the map's corners, away from the
buildings the player paid for, reads as broken rather than as decoration.

**G-3 — The farm's `decor` tile layer is empty.** Zero non-zero gids: no grass tufts, no flowers, no
stones. `ROADMAP.md:6013` already calls this "the single biggest remaining visual win" and it is
right — the ground is a flat `#79BF56` field. **Caution:** the layer sits at `DEPTH.decor`, *below*
every world sprite, so only genuinely flat scatter belongs there; anything with height needs to be a
`DecorPiece` so it sorts on its feet.

**G-4 — No particles and no tweens anywhere.** `grep` confirms zero `add.particles` and zero
`tweens.add` in the client. A three-particle dust puff on a hoe strike, a splash on watering, and a
small pop on harvest would cost very little and are the difference between "the state changed" and
"I did something".

**G-5 — The interior floor is a large empty diagonal hatch.** The room is 12×10 with nothing in it
and a strong repeating stripe that reads as placeholder. A rug on the floor by default, or a subtler
floor tile, would help a lot.

**G-6 — The player has no idle animation variation and buildings have no ambient life.** The maple
trees animate; nothing else does. Smoke from the farmhouse chimney is one sprite.

## J. Definition of done

Tillhaven is ready to put in front of players when:

1. **Nothing in the world draws over the character incorrectly** — plots, decor, furniture,
   buildings and NPCs all sort on feet-Y, with tests pinning the invariant. *(Met for §E's
   criteria; T-18.08 finishes the tall-furniture case.)*
2. **The game fills the screen it is given** at every viewport ≥1024px, at an integer zoom.
3. **Everything drawn as walkable is walkable**, asserted by a test over `OBJECT_TILES`, not by eye.
4. **Every purchased thing is visibly where it belongs** — animals beside their buildings, tiers
   that change the picture, or the tier is not sold.
5. **No control is a dead end**: no disabled button without a reason, no mode that can be switched
   on into doing nothing, no key that does two things at once.
6. **A new player reaches their second harvest without being told what to do** and has something to
   spend gold on at every point in the first hour.
7. **Every action has feedback at the point of contact**, not only in a toast.
8. **The revenue path exists**: VIP is purchasable, or the product decision to defer it is written
   down.
9. **Dead code is gone or wired up** — nothing ships that typechecks but can never run.
10. **The suite stays green** (1,432 tests today) and a full playthrough produces zero console
    errors, which it currently does.

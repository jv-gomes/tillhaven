Everyone whose work you are looking at.

Tillhaven is drawn almost entirely in someone else's hand. The farm, the animals, the crops, the buildings, the character and every frame of the interface come from one pixel-art pack, and the type is two open-licensed faces. Crediting them is not a courtesy here — it is a condition of both licences, and this page is where it is met.

The art pack permits commercial use and modification, and forbids resale or redistribution of the assets themselves. The verbatim terms for everything are recorded in ATTRIBUTION.md in the repository.
